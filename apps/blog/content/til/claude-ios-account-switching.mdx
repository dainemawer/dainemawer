---
title: The Claude iOS app can switch accounts now
date: '2026-10-14'
tags:
  - claude
---

The Claude iOS app now supports account switching. Until recently, moving between a personal and a work account meant signing out and back in every time.

That was a real friction point if you use Claude for both. Switching is now a tap instead of a re-login, which makes the mobile app far more practical alongside Claude Code on the desktop.
